Phase 5: Launch Operations

Month 4-5

Service Launch

$2K - $5K marketing 2 weeks High Priority
  • Begin accepting transportation requests
  • Start with limited geographic area and scale gradually
  • Monitor service quality and customer satisfaction
  • Track key performance metrics daily
  • Address operational issues immediately

Launch Goal

Target: 50+ trips per month within first 30 days

Performance Monitoring

$200 - $500 Ongoing High Priority
  • Track vehicle utilization (target: >40% vs 35% industry average)
  • Monitor on-time performance (target: >95%)
  • Measure customer satisfaction (target: >95%)
  • Analyze profit margins monthly (target: 20-30%)
  • Review cash flow weekly
Critical Success Factor: Vehicle utilization above 35% industry average is essential for profitability.

Revenue Optimization

Ongoing Month 1-3 Medium Priority
  • Diversify payer mix beyond Medicaid (target: 60% Medicaid, 40% other)
  • Negotiate premium rates for specialty services
  • Optimize scheduling to maximize trips per vehicle per day
  • Implement dynamic pricing for non-contract services
  • Focus on high-value services (dialysis, specialty appointments)

Break-Even Analysis

Target: Month 2-3 Ongoing monitoring High Priority
  • Achieve 155+ monthly trips per vehicle for break-even
  • Maintain 5-8 daily trips per vehicle average
  • Monitor monthly operating expenses vs revenue
  • Adjust operations based on profitability analysis
  • Plan for 18-24 month full ROI recovery

Break-Even Target

155 trips/month per vehicle = 5-8 trips/day = Break-even operations
