💰 GRANT #1: Vehicle Acquisition Program - $150K per Vehicle

HHS NEMT Vehicle Funding - Deadline: February 1, 2026

⚠️ WHY THIS IS CRITICAL

Vehicle grants provide $150K per wheelchair-accessible van (up to 5 vehicles = $750K total). This covers 80% of vehicle costs with only 20% match required. Missing February 1 deadline = waiting 12 months for next cycle.

🏃‍♂️ IMMEDIATE ACTION (Days 1-3): Grants.gov Registration

STEP-BY-STEP INSTRUCTIONS:
  1. Visit grants.gov TODAY - Create account with business EIN
  2. SAM.gov registration - REQUIRED before applying (takes 3-5 business days)
  3. DUNS Number - Get from Dun & Bradstreet (free, takes 1-2 days)
  4. Business validation - Upload LLC documents, banking info

WHY URGENT: SAM.gov registration takes 3-5 days and is REQUIRED before you can submit any grant application. Start this TODAY.

📋 DAYS 4-14: Grant Application Preparation

REQUIRED DOCUMENTS (Start gathering NOW):
  1. Business Plan - 15-page plan showing:
    • Market analysis for your service area
    • 5-year financial projections
    • Operational plan (routes, scheduling, staffing)
    • Community impact statement
  2. Vehicle Specifications - Get quotes for:
    • Wheelchair-accessible vans (Ford Transit, Chevy Express)
    • ADA compliance equipment
    • GPS tracking systems
    • Communication equipment
  3. Financial Statements:
    • Personal financial statement (if startup)
    • Business bank statements (if existing)
    • Tax returns (3 years)
    • Credit reports

📝 DAYS 15-25: Application Submission

EXACT APPLICATION STEPS:
  1. Find Grant Opportunity - Search grants.gov for "NEMT Vehicle" or "Medical Transportation Equipment"
  2. Download Application Package - Usually SF-424 form + program-specific forms
  3. Complete Forms:
    • SF-424: Application for Federal Assistance
    • Budget narrative (show 20% match funding source)
    • Project narrative (why you need vehicles, community impact)
    • Organizational capability statement
  4. Submit by January 30 - 2 days before deadline for safety

🎯 GRANT SUCCESS SECRETS

Insider tips to maximize your funding chances

📝

Application Quality

  • Use their language - Mirror grant criteria in your responses
  • Quantify everything - "Serve 150 patients/month" not "many patients"
  • Show community need - Include demographic data, health statistics
  • Professional presentation - Clean formatting, no typos, organized
🤝

Partnerships

  • Get letters early - Healthcare facilities take time to respond
  • Specific commitments - "30 patients/month" vs. "support"
  • Diverse partners - Hospitals, clinics, social services, senior centers
  • Local officials - Mayor, county council support letters
💼

Business Credibility

  • Complete legal structure - LLC, EIN, insurance, permits
  • Professional team - Accountant, lawyer, healthcare advisors
  • Operational readiness - Facility, staff plan, technology
  • Financial stability - Personal/business credit, cash reserves
📊

Financial Planning

  • Conservative projections - Better to under-promise, over-deliver
  • Multiple scenarios - Show best/worst case planning
  • Detailed budgets - Line-item costs, justified expenses
  • Sustainability plan - How you'll continue without grants
