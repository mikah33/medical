💰 THE CASH FLOW GAP

THE PROBLEM: You provide services in Month 3, but don't get paid until Month 4-5. Meanwhile, you're paying insurance, fuel, salaries, and vehicle payments every month.

THE SOLUTION: Plan for 6+ months of operating expenses BEFORE expecting steady revenue. Most NEMT failures happen in months 4-8 due to cash flow gaps.
