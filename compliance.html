🚨 CRITICAL: NEMT Provider Enrollment - DUE JAN 15

This is your #1 priority - without this, you cannot receive Medicaid payments

⚠️ WHY THIS IS ABSOLUTELY CRITICAL

NEMT Provider Enrollment with SC DHHS is REQUIRED to receive Medicaid payments, which represent 60-80% of your revenue. Missing the January 15, 2026 deadline means waiting 6-12 months for the next enrollment period = $0 revenue during that time.

📋 STEP 1: Gather Required Documents (TODAY)

REQUIRED DOCUMENTS CHECKLIST:
  1. LLC Formation Documents
    • Articles of Organization from SC Secretary of State
    • Operating Agreement (if multi-member LLC)
    • WHY NEEDED: Proves legal business entity
  2. Federal EIN (Tax ID)
    • EIN assignment letter from IRS
    • Get from IRS.gov (takes 1 day)
    • WHY NEEDED: Required for all federal/state business
  3. Commercial Insurance Certificates
    • General Liability: $1M minimum
    • Commercial Auto: $1M bodily injury, $500K property
    • Workers' Compensation (if employees)
    • WHY NEEDED: Protects patients and meets state requirements
  4. Background Checks
    • FBI fingerprint background check for all owners
    • State criminal history from SLED
    • Get from SLED office or approved vendor
    • WHY NEEDED: Patient safety requirement

📞 STEP 2: Contact SC DHHS IMMEDIATELY

CALL TODAY: (803) 898-2500

PHONE SCRIPT: "I need to apply for NEMT Provider Enrollment before the January 15 deadline. Can you email me the application package and expedite my processing? This is urgent."

ASK FOR THESE SPECIFIC ITEMS:
  • NEMT Provider Enrollment Application (Form 400)
  • Provider Standards Manual
  • Required insurance coverage details
  • Background check requirements
  • Vehicle certification requirements
  • Expedited processing options

BACKUP EMAIL: ProviderServices@scdhhs.gov with subject "URGENT: NEMT Provider Enrollment Application Request - January 15 Deadline"

📝 STEP 3: Complete Application (Days 2-3)

APPLICATION COMPLETION CHECKLIST:
  1. Business Information Section
    • Legal business name (exactly as on LLC docs)
    • DBA name (if different)
    • Business address and mailing address
    • Federal EIN and State Tax ID
  2. Service Area Declaration
    • Counties you will serve (start with 1-3 rural counties)
    • Types of transport (ambulatory, wheelchair, stretcher)
    • Hours of operation (24/7 recommended)
  3. Vehicle Information
    • Vehicle identification numbers
    • Insurance policy numbers
    • ADA compliance certifications
    • Annual inspection certificates
  4. Driver Qualifications
    • CDL information for all drivers
    • DOT medical certificates
    • Background check results
    • Training certifications

🏢 LEGAL BUSINESS STRUCTURE - Complete in 2-3 Days

Required BEFORE you can apply for NEMT provider enrollment

💡 WHY LLC IS RECOMMENDED

LLC provides personal asset protection, tax flexibility, and meets all NEMT provider requirements. Corporation adds complexity without additional benefits for most NEMT businesses.

📄 STEP 1: Form LLC with SC Secretary of State

ONLINE FILING (FASTEST - Same Day):
  1. Go to sos.sc.gov → Business Filings → Online Services
  2. Choose "File Articles of Organization"
  3. Business Name Requirements:
    • Must end with "LLC" or "Limited Liability Company"
    • Cannot conflict with existing business names
    • Recommended format: "[Your Name] Medical Transport LLC"
  4. Registered Agent:
    • Must be SC resident OR SC business
    • Can be yourself (if SC resident) or hire service ($150/year)
    • Receives official legal documents
  5. Filing Fee: $110 (pay online)
  6. Processing: Same day for online filings

🆔 STEP 2: Obtain Federal EIN (Same Day)

ONLINE APPLICATION AT IRS.GOV:
  1. Go to irs.gov → Business → Apply for EIN
  2. Choose "Apply Online Now"
  3. Required Information:
    • LLC legal name (exactly as filed with state)
    • Business address
    • Responsible party name and SSN
    • Business type: "Limited Liability Company"
    • Reason for applying: "Started new business"
  4. Business Activity: "Ambulance Services and Rescue Services"
  5. PRINT AND SAVE: EIN assignment letter immediately

🏦 STEP 3: Open Business Bank Account (Next Day)

REQUIRED DOCUMENTS TO BRING:
  • Articles of Organization from SC Secretary of State
  • EIN assignment letter from IRS
  • Operating Agreement (if multi-member LLC)
  • Personal ID (driver's license)
  • Initial deposit ($500-1000 recommended)
BANK RECOMMENDATIONS:
  1. Local Community Banks (better relationships)
  2. First Citizens Bank (SBA lending partner)
  3. TD Bank (good commercial services)

WHY URGENT: Business bank account required for insurance payments and grant applications. Must be established before NEMT provider enrollment.

🛡️ INSURANCE COVERAGE - Critical for Operations

Specific coverage requirements and where to get them

⚠️ WHY INSURANCE CANNOT BE DELAYED

Insurance certificates are required for NEMT provider enrollment AND to legally operate vehicles. No insurance = no legal operation = no revenue.

🚗 COMMERCIAL AUTO INSURANCE (REQUIRED)

MINIMUM COVERAGE REQUIREMENTS:
  • Bodily Injury: $1,000,000 per accident
  • Property Damage: $500,000 per accident
  • Comprehensive & Collision: Actual cash value
  • Uninsured Motorist: $1,000,000
SPECIALIZED INSURERS TO CALL:
  1. Progressive Commercial
    • Phone: (800) 777-2264
    • Specializes in medical transport
    • Quote in 1-2 days
  2. GEICO Commercial
    • Phone: (800) 833-5463
    • Competitive rates for medical transport
  3. State Farm Business
    • Contact local agent
    • Good for bundling multiple policies

PHONE SCRIPT: "I need commercial auto insurance for a medical transportation business with wheelchair-accessible vehicles. I need $1M bodily injury and $500K property damage coverage. Can you provide a quote and certificate within 2 days?"

EXPECTED COST: $8,000-15,000 per vehicle annually

🏢 GENERAL LIABILITY INSURANCE (REQUIRED)

COVERAGE REQUIREMENTS:
  • General Liability: $1,000,000 per occurrence
  • Professional Liability: $1,000,000 (recommended)
  • Product/Completed Operations: $2,000,000 aggregate
WHERE TO GET QUOTES:
  • Same insurers as commercial auto (bundle for discount)
  • Progressive, GEICO, State Farm all offer this
  • Request quotes when calling for auto insurance

EXPECTED COST: $2,000-4,000 annually

👷 WORKERS' COMPENSATION (If Hiring Employees)

WHEN REQUIRED:
  • 4 or more employees (including part-time)
  • Any employees in SC = required coverage
  • Contractors/1099 workers = not required
COVERAGE DETAILS:
  • Medical expenses for work-related injuries
  • Lost wage compensation
  • Disability benefits

GET FROM: Same insurer as other policies

EXPECTED COST: $3,000-6,000 annually per employee

🚐 VEHICLE & DRIVER COMPLIANCE

Specific requirements for vehicles and drivers

🚙 VEHICLE REQUIREMENTS CHECKLIST

ADA COMPLIANCE REQUIREMENTS:
  1. Wheelchair Accessibility
    • Rear ramp or side lift
    • Minimum 30" wide entrance
    • 48" minimum interior length
    • Wheelchair securement systems (4-point tie-downs)
  2. Safety Equipment
    • Two-way radio communication
    • GPS tracking system
    • First aid kit
    • Fire extinguisher
    • Emergency contact information
  3. Vehicle Registration
    • Commercial vehicle registration at SC DMV
    • USDOT number (if over 10,000 lbs or interstate)
    • Annual safety inspection

WHERE TO GET ADA CERTIFICATION

Contact vehicle dealer or conversion company to provide ADA compliance documentation. Must be included with NEMT application.

👨‍💼 DRIVER QUALIFICATION REQUIREMENTS

REQUIRED FOR ALL DRIVERS:
  1. Valid Driver's License
    • CDL not required for vehicles under 26,000 lbs
    • Regular license OK for most NEMT vehicles
    • Clean driving record (no DUI in 5 years)
    • No more than 2 moving violations in 3 years
  2. DOT Medical Certificate
    • Required if vehicle seats 16+ passengers
    • Get from DOT-certified medical examiner
    • Cost: $75-150
    • Valid for 2 years
  3. Background Checks
    • State criminal history (SLED)
    • Federal background check
    • Sex offender registry check
    • Drug screening
  4. Training Requirements
    • Wheelchair securement training
    • Defensive driving course
    • First Aid/CPR (recommended)
    • Customer service training
WHERE TO GET BACKGROUND CHECKS:
  • SLED (SC Law Enforcement): (803) 896-7200
  • FBI Background: Through approved channeler
  • Cost: $25-50 per person
  • Processing Time: 3-5 business days
