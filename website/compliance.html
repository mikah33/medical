NEMT Provider Enrollment Process

Detailed steps for becoming an approved NEMT provider

⚠️ Critical Deadline

Applications must be submitted by January 15, 2026 for enrollment in the next period. Missing this deadline means waiting 6-12 months for the next enrollment opportunity.

Application Requirements

  • Completed SCDHHS Provider Enrollment Application
  • Articles of Incorporation or LLC formation documents
  • Federal Tax ID (EIN) documentation
  • Proof of commercial liability insurance ($1M minimum)
  • Commercial auto insurance certificates
  • Background checks for all owners/officers
  • Driver qualification files for all drivers
  • Vehicle inspection certificates
  • ADA compliance certification

Provider Standards

  • Maintain 24/7 dispatch availability
  • Respond to trip requests within specified timeframes
  • Maintain vehicle cleanliness and safety standards
  • Complete monthly reporting requirements
  • Participate in provider training programs
  • Submit claims within 30 days of service

Ongoing Compliance

  • Annual provider recertification
  • Quarterly safety inspections
  • Monthly financial reporting
  • Continuous driver training and certification
  • Regular HIPAA compliance audits
  • Customer satisfaction monitoring

Insurance Requirements

Mandatory insurance coverage for medical transportation

🛡️

Commercial Auto Insurance

Minimum Coverage Requirements:

  • Bodily Injury: $1,000,000 per occurrence
  • Property Damage: $500,000 per occurrence
  • Comprehensive and Collision coverage
  • Uninsured/Underinsured motorist coverage

Estimated Annual Cost: $8,000 - $15,000 per vehicle

🏢

General Liability

Coverage Areas:

  • Slip and fall incidents
  • Property damage claims
  • Personal injury protection
  • Professional liability coverage

Estimated Annual Cost: $2,000 - $4,000

💼

Workers' Compensation

Required for:

  • All employees (drivers, dispatchers)
  • Medical expenses for work injuries
  • Lost wage compensation
  • Disability benefits

Estimated Annual Cost: $3,000 - $6,000

Safety Protocols & Procedures

Essential safety measures for medical transportation

👨‍⚕️

Driver Qualifications

  • Valid Commercial Driver's License (CDL)
  • DOT Medical Certificate
  • Clean driving record (no DUI, limited violations)
  • Background check clearance
  • Defensive driving course completion
  • First Aid/CPR certification (recommended)
  • Wheelchair securement training
🚐

Vehicle Standards

  • ADA-compliant wheelchair accessibility
  • Proper wheelchair securement systems
  • Two-way radio communication
  • GPS tracking capability
  • Emergency medical kit
  • Daily safety inspection logs
  • Regular maintenance schedule
📋

Documentation Requirements

  • Trip manifests and logs
  • Vehicle inspection reports
  • Driver qualification files
  • Insurance certificates
  • HIPAA compliance documentation
  • Incident and accident reports
  • Customer satisfaction records
