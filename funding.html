NEMT Direct Business Grants

Federal grants specifically for medical transportation companies

NEMT Vehicle Acquisition Grant

Up to $150,000 per Vehicle | Total Program: $500 Million

Grant Details

  • Purpose: Purchase wheelchair-accessible vehicles
  • Amount: Up to $150,000 per vehicle
  • Match Required: 20% business contribution
  • Vehicles Covered: Up to 5 vehicles per business

Eligible Expenses

  • Wheelchair-accessible vans/buses
  • Medical equipment and safety systems
  • GPS tracking and dispatch technology
  • Driver training and certification costs

Eligibility Requirements

  • Enrolled NEMT provider in South Carolina
  • Serve Medicaid beneficiaries
  • Demonstrate financial stability
  • Commit to 5-year service area coverage
  • Maintain ADA compliance
  • Submit quarterly utilization reports

Application Process

Deadline: February 1, 2026 | Decision: April 2026 | Funding Available: July 2026

Apply at: grants.gov (search "NEMT Vehicle Grant 2026")

Rural NEMT Expansion Grant

$50,000 - $500,000 | Focus: Rural & Underserved Areas

Program Focus

  • Target: Rural counties with limited NEMT access
  • Priority: Dialysis, cancer treatment transport
  • Coverage: 24/7 emergency medical transport
  • Technology: Real-time tracking and coordination

Funding Uses

  • Fleet expansion for rural coverage
  • Technology integration systems
  • Staff training and certification
  • Partnership development with healthcare facilities

South Carolina Priority Areas

Bamberg, Barnwell, McCormick, Allendale counties identified as highest priority for rural NEMT expansion funding.

NEMT Technology Innovation Grant

Up to $1,000,000 | Multi-Year Awards Available

Innovation Focus Areas

  • AI-Powered Scheduling: Optimize route efficiency
  • Care Coordination: Integration with EHR systems
  • Patient Experience: Mobile apps and real-time updates
  • Data Analytics: Predictive modeling for demand

Technology Requirements

  • Demonstrate measurable cost savings
  • Improve patient satisfaction scores
  • Reduce missed appointments by 25%+
  • Enable data sharing with healthcare providers

SBA Loans for NEMT Businesses

Low-interest government-backed loans for medical transportation companies

SBA 504 Loan Program

Up to $5.5 Million | 3.5-4.5% Interest
  • Purpose: Vehicle purchase, facilities, equipment
  • Down Payment: Only 10% required
  • Term: 10-25 years
  • Use: Medical vehicles, dispatch center, maintenance facility
Preferred for NEMT

SBA 7(a) Express Loan

Up to $500,000 | Fast Approval (36 hours)
  • Purpose: Working capital, equipment, vehicles
  • Approval: 36 hours or less
  • Interest: 11.5-18% (based on creditworthiness)
  • Collateral: Business assets secure the loan
Quick Funding

SBA Microloans

Up to $50,000 | 8-13% Interest
  • Purpose: Small equipment purchases, working capital
  • Term: Up to 6 years
  • Requirements: Less stringent than traditional loans
  • Best for: New NEMT businesses, smaller equipment needs
Startup Friendly

Funding Application Strategy

Maximize your funding success with this strategic approach

Phase 1: Immediate Actions (Next 30 Days)

  • NEMT Provider Enrollment: Submit application by January 15, 2026
  • Business Documentation: Gather financial statements, business plan, insurance
  • Vehicle Grant Application: Prepare for February 1, 2026 deadline
  • SBA Pre-qualification: Meet with approved SBA lenders

Phase 2: Grant Applications (30-60 Days)

  • Rural Expansion Grant: Submit by February 28, 2026
  • Technology Innovation: Prepare application for March deadline
  • State Incentive Programs: Apply for SC-specific funding
  • Training Grants: Apply for workforce development funding

Phase 3: Implementation (60+ Days)

  • Award Notifications: Expected April-June 2026
  • Vehicle Procurement: Order wheelchair-accessible vehicles
  • Technology Implementation: Install dispatch and tracking systems
  • Service Launch: Begin operations with funded equipment

Pro Tips for Application Success

  • Document Everything: Keep detailed records of all communications and applications
  • Apply Early: Submit applications well before deadlines
  • Multiple Sources: Apply for several programs simultaneously
  • Professional Help: Consider hiring a grant writer for large applications
  • Follow Up: Stay in contact with program officers throughout the process
