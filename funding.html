SBA (Small Business Administration) Programs

Loan programs and limited grant opportunities with 2025-2026 program changes.

2025-2026 SBA Changes

New requirements include collateral for loans $50K+ (down from $500K), minimum credit score 165 (up from 155), and 10% equity injection for startups.

7(a) Loans

Up to $5 Million
  • Uses: Real estate, working capital, furniture, supplies
  • Terms: Varies by use (up to 25 years real estate)
  • Fees: 0.25% - 3.75% based on amount and term
  • New Requirement: Collateral required for loans $50,000+

Success Tips

  • Start Early: Many programs have complex application processes
  • Rural Focus: Emphasize rural service delivery in all applications
  • Community Partnerships: Demonstrate healthcare facility support
  • Technology Integration: Highlight innovation and efficiency
  • Multiple Applications: Apply to multiple programs simultaneously

Required Partnerships

  • Healthcare Facilities: Letters of support essential
  • State Agencies: Coordinate with SCDHHS and SCDOT
  • Local Government: County and municipal endorsements
